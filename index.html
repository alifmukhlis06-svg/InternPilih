Everything needed after an offer arrives.

Six focused modules cover decisions, communication, documents, monitoring, company records, and reporting.

Module 1

Authentication & Profile Management

Secure role-based login for Students, Coordinators, and Employers. Manage personal profiles, upload photos, and update account credentials.

Module 2

Internship Dashboard & Offer Management

Centralized dashboard for real-time internship offer tracking. Students record, view, and decide on offers. Coordinators monitor all activities.

Module 3

Professional Communication & Rejection System

Automated rejection template system enables students to formally decline offers. Eliminates ghosting with professional, pre-drafted communications.

Module 4

Internship Placement Monitoring & Verification

Coordinators verify placements, detect duplicate offers, approve uploaded documents, and ensure placement accuracy and compliance.

Module 5

Company Database & Opportunity Management

Centralized database of registered internship companies. Full CRUD for coordinators. Students can browse companies and available opportunities.

Module 6

Notification & Reporting System

Automated reminders, deadline alerts, coordinator announcements, and exportable placement reports with visual analytics dashboards.

Built around real internship decisions.

UiTM manages thousands of students across faculties every semester. The existing semi-manual process involving spreadsheets and physical documents creates delays, communication gaps, and offer hoarding problems where students hold multiple offers without deciding, leaving employers waiting and blocking other students.

Eliminates Ghosting

Automated rejection templates ensure no employer is left without a formal response.

Real-time Monitoring

Coordinators see all offer activities instantly, flagging at-risk students early.

Role-based Access

RBAC ensures each user only sees and does what is appropriate for their role.

Data-driven Reports

Visual analytics and exportable reports support better decision-making each semester.
