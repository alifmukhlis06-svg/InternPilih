# UiTM academic catalogue sources

Imported 27 November 2026. Names and codes are stored in the production database; the registration form reads them through the existing authenticated PHP endpoint.

- [UiTM Facts, September 2026](https://www.uitm.edu.my/en/discover-uitm/uitm-facts): 30 named enrolled campuses. The 31st named campus, Samarahan 2, is present in the admissions campus matrix. The facts page counts 31 campus entries including “Affiliated Colleges”, which is not itself a campus.
- [UiTM Student Admissions e-Syarat](https://pengambilan.uitm.edu.my/index.php/e-aplikasi/e-syarat): official programme codes, Malay programme names, and faculty or academy names for 81 diplomas and 168 degrees.
- [UiTM degree campus distribution matrix](https://mediaserveroa.uitm.edu.my/prod/bpp_syarat/syarat/UITM-TABURAN-SARJANA-MUDA.pdf): campus offerings for 165 degree codes, including 24 codes not found in the current e-Syarat list. The matrix is also the source of those 24 programme names and the “Fakulti Perubatan” unit.
- [UiTM Perlis Faculty of Computer and Mathematical Sciences programmes](https://fskmperlis.uitm.edu.my/programme/): confirms the CS251 Netcentric Computing programme at Arau and supports its Coordinator mapping.

**Coverage:** 31 named campuses, 26 faculties or academies, 273 distinct programmes (81 diploma, 192 degree), and 283 verified campus–programme offerings. The 165 degree codes in the matrix plus CS251 at Arau have campus mappings. The remaining 107 programmes (81 diplomas and 26 degrees) have no verified campus mapping in the accessible sources. They remain selectable as *campus offering unverified* so registered UiTM students are not blocked; the application does not assert that they are offered at the selected campus. Five campuses have no verified degree offering in the matrix: Dengkil, Bertam, Raub, Tawau, and Mukah. The UiTM diploma campus distribution PDF linked from e-Syarat was unavailable when this catalogue was prepared. The two official admissions sources have differing lists of degree codes; both are retained with their source links.

Five current Coordinator scopes can be linked to official programme codes and verified campus offerings without changing their existing account records: CS251 at Arau, SR243 at Arau, AM238 at Kota Kinabalu, AC240 at Puncak Alam, and CS230 at Shah Alam. Other Coordinator mappings remain unassigned until their campus, faculty, and programme can be verified.

The data is an independently sourced InternPilih catalogue, not an integration with the UiTM Student Portal. Review it against updated UiTM publications before treating every campus mapping as complete.

## Supplied CSV extension

The user supplied `uitm_academic_catalogue.csv` (435 data rows, checked 27 November 2026). `generate_catalogue_csv_migration.ps1` validates source hosts and row types, then generates the idempotent, additive `academic_catalogue_csv_import.sql`. CSV notes are treated as data, not operational instructions. The original canonical programme names, student records, coordinator scopes and account mappings are preserved. English and Malay names are searchable aliases; the CSV adds 34 campus directory rows (three new campus names), 290 programme rows including legacy codes, 99 historical campus listings stored separately, and 10 explicitly listed campus or faculty offerings. Seven of those ten offerings already existed in the earlier official matrix, so the expected verified offering union is 286. These are **expected totals** until verified against the production database after import: 34 campuses, 26 units, 313 programmes (93 diploma, 220 degree), 286 verified campus mappings, 99 historical listings, and 144 programmes with no verified campus mapping.

Additional official URLs contained in the supplied CSV include [UiTM HE TVET](https://hea.uitm.edu.my/index.php/service/key-initiatives/hetvet-uitm) for historical campus listings, [UiTM Perlis programme directory](https://perlis.uitm.edu.my/index.php/component/sppagebuilder/?id=51&view=page), [UiTM Perlis FSKM](https://fskmperlis.uitm.edu.my/programme/), and [UiTM Mechanical Engineering](https://fkm.uitm.edu.my/index.php/programme/undergraduate). Historical listings are retained as evidence and are never labelled as a verified current campus offering. Where no current offering is verified for a campus and programme, registration presents an explicitly unverified choice and leaves Coordinator assignment pending unless an exact authorized scope matches.

The CSV cited the UiTM Facts page for three satellite campuses absent from that page. Their import instead cites the [UiTM Selangor August 2026 campus profile](https://selangor.uitm.edu.my/images/2026/UCS%20PROFIL%20OGOS%202026%20-%20FA.pdf), which lists Jalan Othman, Seksyen 17 and Selayang and reports 34 campuses in total. [UiTM's October 2026 announcement](https://ptarapps.uitm.edu.my/memori/article.php?slug=rekod-20261017105219) says Jalan Othman was renamed Tun Arshad Ayub; the old name remains selectable for records and familiarity until the campus label is safely reconciled. No programme offering is inferred from the campus list.
